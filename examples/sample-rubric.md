# A3 Video Pitch Rubric

## Problem & users (10 pts)
Clearly states who the target users are and what problem they face. Grounded in needfinding.

## Solution demo (20 pts)
Walks through the core task flow end to end. Explains what the user does at each step.

## Design rationale (10 pts)
Explains at least one design decision and the evidence behind it.

## Insight / learning (5 pts)
Shares a surprising finding from testing or interviews.

## Delivery (5 pts)
Clear, well paced, under the time limit.

Total: 50
